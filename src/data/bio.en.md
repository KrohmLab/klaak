Immersed in the world of psytrance for over 12 years, Klaak is the fruit of a revelation: Hitech. A style that's explosive, euphoric and full of positive energy, it inspired him to move from organization to the stage. Co-founder of the Outrance collective, a key player in Lyon's psytrance scene, he has spent years thrilling dancefloors while scouring festivals across France and Europe, soaking up the essence of the biggest psytrance scenes to nourish his musical universe. Today, it's behind the turntables that he wants to infuse his energy, distilling a groovy, happy and supercharged hitech.
